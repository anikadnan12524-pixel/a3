About Daily Food Guide

An independent guide that helps everyday cooks plan, shop and cook with less stress.

Our story

Why a daily food guide?

For most households, the hardest part of cooking isn’t the cooking. It’s deciding what to make every single day, remembering what’s in the fridge and finding the energy after a long day. Daily Food Guide exists to make those everyday decisions easier.

We share simple weekly meal plans, reliable everyday recipes and practical tools for planning dinners, building balanced plates and storing food safely. Our recipes are cooked in a home kitchen and our storage and safety advice follows widely used food safety guidance.

Daily Food Guide is independent. We don’t sell food or kitchen products and aren’t paid to recommend brands. We also don’t give medical or nutrition advice; for specific dietary needs, please speak with a qualified professional.
