Recipes

Our everyday recipes

Roasted squash soup with feta

Prep 15 minCook 40 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium butternut squash, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 900 ml vegetable stock
  • 60 g feta, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp toasted pumpkin seeds

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the squash, onion and garlic with the oil, cumin, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast for 30–35 minutes until soft and caramelised at the edges.
  3. Tip into a pan with the stock, simmer for 5 minutes, then blend until smooth.
  4. Serve topped with feta and pumpkin seeds.

Tip: Make a double batch: it freezes well for up to three months.

Weeknight penne with meat sauce

Prep 10 minCook 30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 350 g penne
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 400 g lean minced beef or turkey
  • 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Grated Parmesan to serve

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil for 6 minutes, then add the garlic.
  2. Add the mince and brown it well, breaking it up with a spoon.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, paste and oregano and simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
  4. Meanwhile cook the penne, drain and toss with the sauce. Serve with Parmesan.

Tip: Grated carrot adds sweetness and an easy extra vegetable.

Big walnut, olive and cheese salad

Prep 15 minCook 5 minServes 2

Ingredients

  • 1 head of lettuce or mixed leaves
  • Handful of olives
  • 40 g walnuts, toasted
  • 80 g cheese such as feta or goat’s cheese
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard

Method

  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3–4 minutes, then cool.
  2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice and mustard with a pinch of salt.
  3. Toss the leaves and cucumber with the dressing.
  4. Top with olives, walnuts and crumbled cheese and serve straight away.

Tip: Add a can of drained chickpeas or leftover chicken to make it a full meal.

Making a plan stick

  • Write it down where everyone can see it, like the fridge door.
  • Prep on day one: wash salad, cook a pot of grains and chop onions.
  • Be flexible: swap days when plans change rather than abandoning the list.
  • Use the fridge checker to decide what to eat first.
